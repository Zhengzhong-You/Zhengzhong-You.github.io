The [official UMFM 2026 workshop program](https://rsvp.withgoogle.com/events/sigspatial-2026-umfm-workshop) is now available. Our accepted vision paper, [*Towards Routing Foundation Models for Commercial Vehicles*](/papers/routing-foundation-models/), by Bo Tang, Yunfei Ma, and Zhengzhong Ricky You, is scheduled for the Vision Papers session on November 3, 2026, in Riverside, California, as part of ACM SIGSPATIAL 2026.

Read the full paper: [PDF](/papers/routing-foundation-models-umfm-2026.pdf).
